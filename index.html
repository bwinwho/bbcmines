What we mine

China Clay

Kaolin

A soft, white hydrous aluminium silicate. High fusion temperature, chemically inert, bright on firing. The whiteness in whiteware.

  • Sanitaryware
  • Tiles
  • Tableware
  • Refractories
  • Electrical Porcelain
Technical data

Ball Clay

Plastic Clay

Fine-grained and sedimentary — kaolinite with mica and quartz. The plasticity and green strength that let a body be thrown, cast and handled long before it sees a kiln.

  • Sanitaryware
  • Tiles
  • Tableware
  • Refractories
  • Electrical Porcelain
Technical data

Feldspar

Potash & Soda

The flux. It lowers the vitrification point and forms the glassy phase that makes a fired body dense, hard and translucent.

  • Sanitaryware
  • Tiles
  • Tableware
  • Glass
  • Glazes & Frits
Technical data

Quartz

Silica

Crystalline silica — the skeleton. It holds dimensional stability through firing and cooling, controls thermal expansion, and carries the body's hardness.

  • Sanitaryware
  • Tiles
  • Glass
  • Foundry & Abrasives
  • Refractories
